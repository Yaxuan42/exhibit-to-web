我的命题应当是以如下方式来起阐明作用的：任何理解我的人，当他用这些命题为梯级而超越了它们时，就会终于认识到它们是无意义的。可以说，在登上高处之后他必须把梯子扔掉。 My propositions serve as elucidations in the following way: anyone who understands me eventually recognizes them as nonsensical, when he has used them — as steps — to climb beyond them. He must, so to speak, throw away the ladder after he has climbed up it.
维特根斯坦 · 逻辑哲学论 §6.54 Wittgenstein · Tractatus §6.54
